TabSweep

Cookies gone when the tab is closed

TabSweep is a Chrome extension that deletes a site's cookies shortly after you close its last tab. Sites you want to stay signed in to go on a whitelist and are never touched.

What it does

  • Deletes the cookies of closed sites after a delay you choose (30 seconds by default).
  • Optional deeper cleaning: localStorage, IndexedDB, cache storage and service workers.
  • Whitelist for sites to keep, with no limit on the number of sites.
  • Greylist for sites to keep until the browser restarts, or for a number of days.
  • Checks itself: after each cleanup it reads the cookies again and shows how many were really deleted.

Safe from the first minute

  • Sites that already had cookies when you installed TabSweep are left alone until you choose what to do with them, so you are not signed out everywhere on day one.
  • "Clean this site now" affects only the site you are on, and is switched off for whitelisted sites.
  • Bulk cleanups show what will be deleted and ask you to confirm.

Private by design

TabSweep works on your device. It has no server, no account, no analytics and no ads, and it sends nothing anywhere.
